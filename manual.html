Playbook Enterprise User Manual

Welcome to Playbook Enterprise.

Playbook is the world’s most advanced Client-Side Distributed AI Grading Engine. Built specifically for educational institutions, it operates on a secure "Zero-Hallucination Architecture," ensuring deterministic, fair, and highly accurate evaluation of handwritten and typed exams.

Crucially, Playbook is designed around the Human-in-the-Loop philosophy. The AI acts as your tireless teaching assistant—doing the heavy lifting of reading, scoring, and writing micro-lessons—but you, the educator, always hold the final authority.

This manual will guide you step-by-step through the Playbook ecosystem.

1. Introduction to Playbook Enterprise

Playbook maximizes data privacy and processing speed. Student exam images are securely stored in our cloud storage buckets and processed securely, before being evaluated directly via our secure Enterprise AI models using your institution's dedicated API key.

Core Capabilities

  • Bulk PDF Scanning: Upload a single PDF containing an entire class's exams. Our visual engine automatically detects blank separator pages to perfectly split the document into individual student submissions.
  • The 4 Tiers of Evaluation: The AI evaluates answers based on Semantic Equivalence (understanding meaning, not just keywords), Proportional Math (awarding partial credit), the Fatal Flaw rule (zero marks for fundamentally wrong science), and Diagram Amnesty (grading text over messy sketches).
  • Micro-Lesson Feedback: For every question, the AI writes personalized, 3-sentence actionable feedback addressing the student directly, explaining exactly what they missed and how to improve.
  • Instant Enterprise Analytics: Generate beautiful class-wide insights, download CSV score reports for your LMS, and instantly export individually branded PDF feedback sheets for every student.

2. Getting Started: Authentication & Setup

Playbook utilizes a secure Dual-Interface Architecture. Your institution's IT Administrator handles the backend API configuration, meaning you can focus entirely on grading.

  • Register via SSO: If your IT Admin has sent you an invite link, click it to register. You must register using your official university email domain.
  • Sign In: Navigate to the Playbook Dashboard and log in securely.
  • The Dashboard: Your command center. Here, you will see a high-level overview of your "Exams Graded," your "Pending Reviews," and a historical list of all your recent grading sessions.

3. How to Upload and Process Exams

Preparing and uploading your exams is the most critical step to ensure perfect grading.

Step A: Preparing Physical Exams

Before scanning your physical stack of exams into a PDF, you must insert exactly one blank sheet of paper between Student A's exam and Student B's exam. Our "Blank Page Detection" algorithm scans the microscopic pixel data of every page to automatically group students correctly.

Step B: The Marking Scheme

A rigorous marking scheme guarantees flawless AI execution.

  1. Go to the Upload Exams tab.
  2. Paste your raw marking scheme into the text box.
  3. Optional but Highly Recommended: Click "Optimize Scheme". Our Smart Pre-Processor will automatically rewrite your rubric into the strict "Playbook Standard Format," preserving every alternative answer while strictly enforcing question hierarchy.

Step C: Start Playbook Grading

  1. Enter a clear Session Name (e.g., "Biology 101 - Midterm").
  2. Enter the Total Exam Marks (the absolute maximum score a student can achieve). Note: Playbook strictly delegates math to your browser to prevent AI hallucinations, so this number must be accurate.
  3. Upload your bulk PDF and click "Start Playbook Grading".

The Live Processing Queue: Playbook will begin chunking and grading the exams locally. If your internet drops, do not panic! Our local IndexedDB queue will pause safely, allowing you to click "Resume" when your connection returns.

4. Understanding AI Grading & The 'Review & Override' Process

Playbook features a "Live Review Theater." As soon as the first student's exam is graded, a button will appear allowing you to open the Review interface in a new tab while the rest of the class processes in the background.

The Review & Override interface is where the Human-in-the-Loop philosophy takes over. We mandate that you review the AI's logic before finalizing grades.

Navigating the Review Screen

  • The Single-Pane Layout: You will see each question neatly stacked.
  • Playbook Justification: Located immediately below the score. This is the AI's internal "Chain-of-Thought" mathematical reasoning. Read this first to verify why the AI awarded specific points based on your rubric.
  • Constructive Feedback: The actual micro-lesson that the student will read.

How to Override

If you disagree with the AI's assessment:

  1. Click the "Override" button next to any question.
  2. Adjust the numerical score or edit the text of the Constructive Feedback directly.
  3. Click "Save". The system will instantly recalculate the student's Total Score deterministically.

Once you are satisfied with the entire exam, click "Finalize Review". The student's status will change from Needs Review to Completed.

5. Navigating Analytics & Reports

Once all exams in a session are finalized, navigate to the Analytics tab to view your class's performance.

Class Insights

  • Stats Grid: Instantly view the Total Students, Average Score, Highest Score, and the Lowest Performing Question to guide your next lecture.
  • Score Distribution: A visual chart showing how the class performed across different grade brackets (A, B, C, F).

Exporting Enterprise Reports

Scroll down to the Individual Results table to manage your data:

  • View Feedback: Click this button next to any student to open a sleek slide-out drawer. You can instantly read their total score, grade, and full AI evaluation without leaving the page.
  • Download CSV: Export a clean spreadsheet of all student registration numbers and final scores, ready to be uploaded directly into Canvas, Blackboard, or your university LMS.
  • Download PDF: Click this next to an individual student to instantly generate a beautifully formatted, enterprise-branded PDF containing their specific grades and micro-lessons.
  • Download All Feedback (PDF): Click this master button at the top of the screen to automatically compile the entire class into a single, multi-page professional PDF document—perfect for printing and handing back to students.
